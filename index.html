Haven X Ethics Check & Responsible Use

Model Card & Privacy Safeguard Commitments

1. Zero Personal Data Tracking

Haven X strictly aggregates spatial telemetry data using K-Anonymity and differential privacy algorithms. No Personally Identifiable Information (PII) such as names, device IDs, or personal contact info is ever stored or analyzed.

2. Fair Scheduling & Worker Rights

The AI workforce engine functions purely as a prescriptive advisory tool. Human managers must approve shift adjustments. The algorithm includes guardrails that enforce guaranteed minimum weekly shift hours and provide 14-day advance notice to protect employee stability.

3. Algorithmic Equity Across Municipalities

Foot traffic scoring models are normalized across all 39 Palm Beach County municipalities to prevent algorithmic bias that could disproportionately divert tourist traffic away from small, minority-owned, or underrepresented local businesses.
